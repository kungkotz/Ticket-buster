One-time setup

This app uses Claude AI to read your screenshots and pull out the train details automatically. To do that it needs an API key — think of it like a password that lets the app talk to Claude. It's free to get, and your key is only stored in your browser.

1
Go to console.anthropic.com/settings/keys and sign up (free)
2
Click "Create Key", give it any name, copy the key — it looks like sk-ant-api03-…
3
Paste it below and press Continue
